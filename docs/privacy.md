# Lynko — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-16**

Lynko is a local-only companion app. Everything happens between your phone and
your paired desktop over your own home network. There is no cloud service, no
account, and no tracking or analytics.

## What Lynko collects

Nothing. Lynko has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics SDK, no ad SDK,
and no crash-reporting service. It never sends your personal data anywhere
outside your local network.

All settings you make (such as your pairing PIN) are stored only on your own
device.

## Permissions, and what each one is used for

| Permission / capability | Why Lynko needs it |
|---|---|
| **Screen capture (media projection)** | Only while you are actively mirroring your screen. Capture stops when you stop mirroring or disconnect. |
| **Accessibility service** | Only to carry gestures typed or performed on your paired desktop to your phone (taps, swipes, typing). It reads no other app's content. |
| **Notification listener** | Only to display your phone's notifications on the paired desktop while you are connected. |
| **Storage (media / files)** | Only when you share or save a file through Lynko. |
| **Microphone / audio capture** | Only when you press Audio on the desktop, and only to stream the device's playback audio to that paired desktop. Lynko uses the playback-capture API, not the microphone. |
| **Network access (internet, Wi-Fi state)** | To find and connect to your paired desktop over your local Wi-Fi network. |

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings.

## Children

Lynko is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.
