# Lynko — Myket listing (English)

## App name
**Lynko**

## Category
Tools

## Short description
Full control of your Android phone from your PC. No cable, no cloud.

## Full description
Lynko shows your Android phone's screen on your computer and lets you control
it completely: tap, swipe, type, open apps, send files, hear your phone's
audio and see its notifications — all over the Wi-Fi network you already share.

No USB debugging, no cable, no account and no cloud. Your phone is found
automatically, confirmed with a 4-digit PIN, and fully controllable from the
very first frame.

**Features**
• Mirror your phone screen to your desktop
• Full control with mouse and keyboard: tap, swipe, scroll, type
• Send files from your computer to your phone
• Sync clipboard both ways
• See phone notifications on your desktop, with quick reply
• Play your phone's audio through your computer speakers
• Runs on your local network — your data never leaves it

**Privacy**
Lynko does everything between your phone and your computer on your home
network. No cloud, no account, no tracking and no analytics. The full privacy
text and a permission-by-permission explanation live inside the app under
Settings → Privacy policy.
